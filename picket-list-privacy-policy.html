Picket List privacy policy.

Picket List is a duty roster app built by Seventh Boar Development. This page explains what the app does — and doesn't do — with your information.

What the app collects

Picket List does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data outside your device. Operation names, personnel names, duty times, and commander notes you enter stay on your device only.

How your data is stored

Everything you enter is saved locally using your device's standard app storage. Nothing is uploaded to a server, and Seventh Boar Development has no access to it.

Share & copy

The Share and Copy buttons hand your roster text to your device's own share sheet or clipboard only when you tap them. Picket List does not send this data anywhere on its own.

Third-party services

Picket List does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind, and does not share data with third parties.

Children's privacy

Picket List is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, regardless of age.

Questions

For privacy-related questions about Picket List, reach out via Admin@seventhboar.com.
