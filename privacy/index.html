Privacy policy.

What Seventh Boar Development collects, why, who we share it with, and how you can see it, correct it or have it deleted. Last updated 5 November 2026.

Who we are

Seventh Boar Development is an independent web studio in Brisbane, Queensland, Australia. We build websites and apps for businesses and brands. This policy covers seventhboar.com and the projects, requests and emails that come from it. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles where they apply to us.

What we collect

  • If you email us or use the contact form: your name, email address and whatever you choose to write.
  • If you send a project request: your name, email and a description of the project, plus anything optional you add, such as a phone number, business name, current website, timeline, links and whether you're interested in a care plan.
  • If you sign up for updates: your email address.
  • If you comment on a devlog entry: the name you type and your comment. Comments are checked before they appear.
  • If we work together: business details, project files, and invoices and payment records.
  • To limit spam: a one-way scrambled version (a hash) of your IP address, stored with requests and sign-ups. It can't be turned back into your IP address, and it's used only to stop one source flooding the forms.

We don't use analytics or advertising trackers on this website at the moment. If we add privacy-friendly analytics, we'll say so here first.

How we use it

  • To reply to your message and to assess and deliver your project, including emailing you updates as your request moves along. Your tracking page is private to you and isn't indexed by search engines.
  • To send devlog and studio news to people who signed up. Every one of those emails has an unsubscribe link.
  • To run, secure and improve the website, and to keep the business and tax records the law requires.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run the site. They handle information only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: stores the database (requests, sign-ups, comments and site content). Our project is hosted in Japan.
  • Resend: sends our emails (confirmations, updates and newsletters).
  • Google: our business email and calendar (Google Workspace), and the fonts the site loads from Google Fonts.
  • GitHub: hosts the website's pages.

Because these providers are overseas, some information is stored or processed outside Australia, including in Japan and the United States. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Cookies and browser storage

We don't use tracking cookies. The site stores small amounts of data in your browser to work properly, such as remembering display settings, speeding up page loading, remembering which comments you've liked, and smoothing the move between pages. Your browser also contacts the services above when a page loads, which is how every website works.

How long we keep it

  • Project requests and client records: as long as needed to deliver the work and answer follow-up questions.
  • Invoices and financial records: at least five years, as tax law requires.
  • Newsletter sign-ups: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.
  • Comments: until you ask us to remove them.

Keeping it safe

Our admin area needs a password and an authenticator code, and the database only lets signed-in staff change anything. Visitors can add comments, sign-ups and requests but can't read other people's. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect what you give us, and we'll tell you if a breach is likely to cause you serious harm.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, and you can unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link in them. Email info@seventhboar.com and we'll respond within 30 days. We may need to confirm who you are first, and some records (like invoices) we're required to keep.

Our website isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Questions and complaints

For any privacy question or complaint, contact us at info@seventhboar.com and we'll do our best to sort it out. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
