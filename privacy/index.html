Privacy policy.

How Seventh Boar Development handles personal information across everything we do: this website, client projects, our apps and games, emails, social media and day-to-day contact. Last updated 5 November 2026.

Who we are and what this covers

Seventh Boar Development is an independent web and app studio based in Brisbane, Queensland, Australia. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Seventh Boar Development.

It applies to all of our business: the website at seventhboar.com, the websites and apps we build for clients, our own apps and games, our emails and phone calls, our social media and marketplace profiles, and in-person contact. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles where they apply to us.

What we collect, and when

  • Enquiries (email, phone, messages, in person, contact form): your name, contact details and whatever you tell us.
  • Project requests: your name, email and project description, plus anything optional you add, such as a phone number, business name, current website, timeline, links and care plan interest.
  • Clients: business and billing details, the material you give us to do the work (logos, photos, text, files), the access details we need (such as your domain or hosting account), our communications with you, and invoices and payment records.
  • Sign-ups and comments: your email address if you sign up for updates, and the name and comment you type if you comment on a devlog entry.
  • Social media and marketplaces: messages, comments and profile details you send us on Instagram, TikTok, X, or work platforms such as Upwork or Airtasker.
  • Our apps and games: each app or game that collects personal information has its own privacy policy. For example, see the Picket List privacy policy.
  • Website visitors: a one-way scrambled version (a hash) of your IP address, stored with requests and sign-ups, to stop spam. It can't be turned back into your IP address.

We don't use analytics or advertising trackers on this website at the moment. If we add privacy-friendly analytics, we'll update this policy first.

How we use it

  • To reply to you, quote, and assess and deliver projects, including emailing you updates as your request moves along. Your tracking page is private to you and isn't indexed by search engines.
  • To invoice, get paid and keep the business and tax records the law requires.
  • To send devlog and studio news to people who signed up. Every one of those emails has an unsubscribe link.
  • To run, secure and improve our website and services, and to handle complaints and disputes.

We don't sell personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Client projects: your customers' information

When we build or look after a website or app for a client, any information it collects from the client's own customers (such as enquiries or sign-ups) belongs to the client. We handle it only on the client's behalf and for the work we've been asked to do. Each client is responsible for their own site's privacy policy, and we're happy to help write one.

We keep client access details and files secure, use them only for the project, and return or delete them when the work or care plan ends, unless the law or a record-keeping need says otherwise.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of providers to run the business. They handle information only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: stores our website database (requests, sign-ups, comments and site content). Our project is hosted in Japan.
  • Resend: sends our website's emails (confirmations, updates and newsletters).
  • Google: our business email, calendar and documents (Google Workspace), and the fonts the website loads from Google Fonts.
  • GitHub: hosts our website's pages.
  • Marketplace and social platforms you contact us through, under their own privacy policies.
  • Our accountant, bank, payment and accounting providers, and insurers: for invoicing, tax and insurance.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our legal rights. Because many of these providers are overseas, some information is stored or processed outside Australia, including in Japan and the United States.

Cookies and browser storage

Our website doesn't use tracking cookies. It stores small amounts of data in your browser to work properly, such as remembering display settings, speeding up page loading, remembering which comments you've liked, and smoothing the move between pages. Your browser also contacts the services above when a page loads, which is how every website works.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and project requests: as long as needed to deal with them, and to answer follow-up questions.
  • Client records and files: for the life of the project or care plan, and for a reasonable time afterwards in case of questions.
  • Invoices and financial records: at least five years, as tax law requires.
  • Newsletter sign-ups: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.
  • Comments: until you ask us to remove them.

Keeping it safe

Our admin systems need a password and an authenticator code, and our website database only lets signed-in staff change anything. Visitors can add comments, sign-ups and requests but can't read other people's. We use reputable providers, keep access details private, and only open information to people who need it. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect what you give us, and we'll tell you if a breach is likely to cause you serious harm.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, and you can unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link in them. Email info@seventhboar.com and we'll respond within 30 days. We may need to confirm who you are first, and some records (like invoices) we're required to keep.

Our website and services aren't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Questions and complaints

For any privacy question or complaint, contact us at info@seventhboar.com and we'll do our best to sort it out. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
